02 // Materiality & Craft

The Architectural Philosophy

VALOIS was constructed with hyper-tactile, self-weathering materials chosen to endure decades of extreme alpine blizzards while exuding warmth within.

Total Interior Footprint: 620 m²
Woodwork

Yakusugi Shou Sugi Ban

Centuries-old Japanese flame-charred larch timber. Fire-resistant, impervious to moisture, and deeply textured with midnight-black iridescence.

Masonry

Vals Mountain Quartzite

Honed monolith blocks quarried locally in Graubünden. Naturally flecked with mica crystals that glimmer under ambient candlelight and low sunlight.

Metallurgy

Hand-Brushed Dark Bronze

Custom forged I-beams, hardware, and motorized window mullions patinated with warm antique wax finishes to reject synthetic glare.

Fenestration

Motorized Acoustic Glass

Triple-glazed heated quartz glass panes rising 8 meters. Silent telescoping mechanisms allow uninterrupted alpine airflow on crisp calm mornings.
